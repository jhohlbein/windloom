    Bestiary

    Skeleton archer
    Bleached bones with a bow, roaming the open sand in groups of two to four from the very start. Fragile, but when one of them spots you, or you strike one, the whole group turns and looses arrows that drop as they fly. More groups rise with every stage of your palace.
    Wyvern
    A lean jade reptile with bat wings and a barbed tail. Quick and fragile, spits red bolts, loses you behind hills.
    Drake
    A heavy rust-red brute with four legs, horns and a clubbed tail. Spots you from further away and spits fireballs. Appears once your Palace stands.
    Kraken
    Lurks under the sea. Surfaces to lob ink and grabs anyone who flies low over it. Can only be hit while surfaced.
    Sandworm
    Tunnels under the dunes. Watch for moving dust, then strike when it rears up and opens its four-jawed maw to spit acid.
    Vharoth
    The ash wyrm. Nests on the highest peak among obsidian spires. Breathes fire and hurls burning boulders. It hunts by sight: break line of sight behind a ridge and it searches where it last saw you before giving up. Hug the ground to slip past its peak unseen. Fireballs nearby give you away. It will not cross your palace's beam.

    Fly through the golden beam above your palace to bank mana and heal quickly. Away from it you mend slowly after a few seconds without taking damage. To replay an island, keep the page address: it ends with the island's seed. · Source on GitHub
